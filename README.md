## What I Build

I specialize in **self-hosted, single-purpose web applications** that prioritize local storage, offline capability, and RTL (right-to-left) language support. Most of my projects share a common architecture: Next.js 16 App Router, Prisma + SQLite for data, IndexedDB for client-side persistence, and shadcn/ui for the interface layer.

Whether it's a streaming platform that scans your local video files, a document editor that exports to PDF with inlined Persian fonts, or a chunked upload system with real-time notifications, the goal is the same: powerful features, zero external dependencies, clean UX.

## Philosophy

> [!NOTE]
> All my projects are built for **private, self-hosted use**. No cloud subscriptions, no third-party auth, no telemetry. Your data stays on your machine.

- **Persian-first** &mdash; RTL layouts, Vazirmatn typography, and Farsi UI text are the default, not an afterthought.
- **Local-first** &mdash; SQLite and IndexedDB over cloud databases. Export formats (PDF, DOC, JSON) are self-contained with inlined fonts and images.
- **Single-purpose** &mdash; Each app does one thing well. No bloat, no feature creep.
- **Dark themes** &mdash; Every project ships with a carefully crafted dark mode designed for extended use.
